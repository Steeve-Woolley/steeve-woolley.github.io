About

I paint in oils from life and from studies made on location. My work returns to the same handful of subjects — light through a window, fruit going soft on a table, the figure held still for twenty minutes.

I also run an animation studio, which means most painting happens early, before the day starts asking for things.

Work is available. For prices, studio visits, or anything else — you@example.com
